Collections

Original photographs, documents, books, periodicals, posters, maps, unpublished manuscripts, and objects. We are cataloguing them for research, citation, and education.

Eight representative holdings, with records that could become a searchable digital catalogue.

More to explore

More of the collection, still being catalogued.

These materials may support future features, online displays, and education projects as cataloguing continues.

01

Children and wartime relief

Photographs of children and orphans, relief publications, and overseas fundraising records trace aid to the youngest victims of war.

02

The Sutton files

Letters about the Tokyo Trial, the University of Nanking, and postwar investigations offer a way to study justice, testimony, memory, and evidence.

03

Medicine, humanitarian aid, and care

Medical instruments, nursing texts, and relief records document treatment, transport, and support beyond the battlefield.

04

Children's publications

Educational and public-facing publications for children across the Pacific brought the Far East into North American classrooms and homes.

05

Late Qing China in photographs

Photographs from the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries record China amid war, treaties, and international photography.

06

Portraits and identity

Portraits, inscriptions, and original mounts offer clues to personal identity, social status, and visual culture.
